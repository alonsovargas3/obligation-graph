## Systematic patterns

1. **Conditions labeled as triggers.** Reference 9, 48 and 49 make a leading "in the event (of/that)" clause the trigger of a contingent label with relation null. That contradicts W5-2 ("Conditions do not bind") and the trigger_span rule. A contingent label with no relation is a red flag: a quoted event with no "within / upon / prior to / after" construction is not a deadline. For 48 and 49 this also hides a real 30-day window that lives in the definitions.
2. **"Upon X" applied inconsistently.** Disjunctive term-end triggers (reference 18 and 29) are labeled conditional_or_compound, while "Upon an Event of Default" (39) and "Upon the occurrence of each ..." (45, 46) are labeled contingent eq. The plan defines compound alternatives as "the earlier/later (to occur) of" only. A reader would treat 18/29 as contingent (term_end), and would treat 39/45/46 as entitlement or liability conditions rather than due-on-event dates. Choose one rule and apply it to all five.
3. **anchor_without_date used for continuous covenants.** Reference 12, 13, 17, 21 and 23 ("during/throughout the Term") use anchor_without_date. The frozen grammar puts "during" and "throughout" under anchor_not_found, and anchor_without_date belongs to a recognized construction whose defined event has no date. A classifier that follows the grammar will lose 5 reason matches here for following the rules.
4. **Trigger spans cross commas.** Reference 19, 22, 29, 30 and 46, and challenge 4, extend past the first comma, against the frozen trigger_span rule. The scorer's trigger IoU threshold is 0.5 (src/og/eval/timing_score.py), so several of these will mark a correct grammar output as a miss (computed IoUs: 0.26, 0.29, 0.42).
5. **Field completeness for business_days is uneven.** Reference 26 and 37 and challenge 2 and 14 fill relation lte, offset and unit business; reference 25 leaves all three null. Null is defensible for 25's two-sided window ("at least ten (10) business days (but no more than one hundred eighty (180) days) prior to"), but the convention should be written down.
6. **Labels go beyond the frozen grammar (correct labels, expected classifier misses, not label errors).** These include "when changes are made" (ref 11), "Once Landlord has rectified" (ref 47), "no later than the thirtieth (30th) day following" (ref 5, ordinal), "no less than thirty (30) days before" (ref 16), "daily updates" (ref 34) and "monthly Base Rent" (ref 30). Expect these to show as kind or trigger misses, and record them as documented grammar limitations under the stop rule. Do not move the labels toward the grammar to close the gap.
7. **Provenance.** Both files still say `spot_checked: 0`, and the plan requires a user spot-check of 10 before freeze. I suggest the spot-check sample include reference 9, 18, 39, 48 and challenge 4.
