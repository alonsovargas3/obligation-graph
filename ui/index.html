Legend: [REDACTED] marks a value redacted in the filing; [BLANK] marks an unfilled placeholder in a form exhibit. Neither is ever inferred. Pending means the documents give no computable due date; it does not mean overdue, and it does not mean there is no obligation. Pending splits into contingent (due relative to a quoted event that has not been recorded), unresolved (timing the system cannot compute; the reason is shown), and untimed (no stated deadline). A strict bound is shown as before the date, never as a due-on date.
